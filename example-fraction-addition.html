Math Bad Habit Breaker Worksheet

Bad Habit to Break:

Students often add fractions by adding the numerators AND the denominators: 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5 (WRONG!). You must find a common denominator first.

Understanding the Pattern:

To add fractions, you need a common denominator. Think of it like adding different sized pieces: you can't add halves and thirds directly. Convert them to the same size pieces (like sixths), then add. For 1/2 + 1/3: Find LCD (6), convert: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Math Bad Habit Breaker

Negative Sign Distribution

🚫 Bad Habit: Forgetting to distribute the negative sign

The Problem: When you have a negative sign in front of parentheses like -(a + b), you must distribute it to BOTH terms inside.

Correct: -(a + b) = -a - b

Common Mistake: -(a + b) = -a + b ❌

📝 Example 1

Simplify: -(3 + x)

Step 1: Distribute the negative to both terms

Step 2: Answer = ______

Correct answer: -3 - x

📝 Example 2

Simplify: -(5 - 2y)

Step 1: Distribute the negative to both terms (remember: minus a negative is plus)

Step 2: Answer = ______

Correct answer: -5 + 2y
